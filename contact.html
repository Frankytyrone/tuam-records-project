Contact & Corrections

This is a source archive rather than a general enquiries desk, and it is not staffed day to day. We do want to hear about genuine errors: a wrong date, a misquoted source, a broken link, or a document that should be added. Every correction is checked against a source, using the same standard set out in our methodology, before anything on the site is changed.

Correction form

Fill in the fields below. When you submit, your report opens as a new item on the project's public GitHub page, ready to send. You will need a free GitHub account to send it; if you don't have one, signing up takes a couple of minutes and costs nothing.

No email address is published for this project, and none is planned. Every report submitted this way stays as a permanent, dated, public record in the project's repository, which fits how this archive is meant to work: checkable, not private.
