Media coverage & corrections

The June 2014 international reporting that first broke the Tuam story is, itself, part of the documentary record — including where outlets, and even Catherine Corless's own on-the-record comments, were later corrected or clarified. These sources are catalogued so that both the original reports and the corrections can be checked side by side, per the site's methodology on tracking corrections rather than hiding them.

IDTitleTypeDateSource
TRP-013 Irish Times interview with Catherine Corless: "Tuam mother and baby home: the trouble with the septic tank story" — Corless states on record that she never used the word "dumped" and that the widely reported certainty that around 800 bodies were in the tank did not come from her Contemporaneous Press Report 7 June 2014 The Irish Times
TRP-014 Associated Press formal correction of its 3 and 8 June 2014 Tuam reports: withdraws the claims that children at the home were denied Roman Catholic baptism and that this reflected Church teaching, after Tuam Archdiocese records showed the home had baptised over 2,000 children; also clarifies that, absent excavation, it was not then possible to know how many remains (if any) the disused septic tank contained Official Correction (Primary Source) 20 June 2014 America Magazine (carries full AP correction text)
